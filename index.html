Privacy Policy

Tap Out 3D: Tied Blocks (Android) · Effective 24 October 2026

Tap Out 3D: Tied Blocks ("the game") is made by Umar Inayat, an independent developer. This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in. The game never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, best scores, daily streak) and settings are stored on your device.
  • The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to see how the game is played (for example, how many players finish level 5) and to measure the game's own ads. The developer only sees totals, never who you are.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's ad software collects some device and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.

Ads and the data Google collects

Ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which collects the following automatically when the game requests or shows an ad:

DataUsed for
IP address, used to estimate your general (approximate) locationAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Interactions such as app launches, taps and video viewsAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performanceAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and app set IDAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention

This data is sent to Google, encrypted in transit, and handled under Google's privacy policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Game analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The game records a few gameplay events with Google Analytics for Firebase: opening the game, starting a mode, finishing or failing a level, finishing the tutorial, using a power, and coming back on a later day. Firebase collects the following with them:

DataUsed for
Interactions: the gameplay events above, app launches and time spentAnalytics, advertising (measuring the game's ads on Google Ads)
Device identifiers: the Firebase app instance ID, and the Android advertising ID where allowedAnalytics, advertising
Approximate location (country and region, estimated from the IP address, which is not stored)Analytics
Diagnostic information: app version, device model and Android versionAnalytics

This data is sent to Google, encrypted in transit, and handled under Firebase's privacy and security terms and Google's Privacy Policy. The developer sees aggregated reports only, and event-level analytics data is deleted after 2 months.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). Before any ad is requested, the game shows Google's consent form where the law requires it. Your answer applies to the ads and to the advertising uses of analytics. You can change it at any time in the game under Settings > Ad privacy options.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the phone).
  • Removing your data. Uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored on your device. The developer keeps no data that identifies you; analytics data held by Google is deleted automatically after the retention period, and for other data held by Google, use the controls described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
