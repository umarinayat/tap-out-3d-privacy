Privacy Policy

VOIDRUNNER: Space Shooter (Android) · Effective 20 October 2026

VOIDRUNNER: Space Shooter ("the game") is published by Umar Inayat, an independent developer. This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in. The game never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress (campaign levels and stars, coins, ships, weapons, cosmetics, missions, achievements, best scores) and settings are stored only on your device. The developer never receives them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's ad software collects some device and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.

Ads and the data Google collects

Ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which collects the following automatically when the game requests or shows an ad:

DataUsed for
IP address, used to estimate your general (approximate) locationAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Interactions such as app launches, taps and video viewsAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performanceAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and app set IDAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention

This data is sent to Google, encrypted in transit, and handled under Google's privacy policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Rewarded ads are always your choice: a video plays only when you tap a button marked AD (for example REVIVE), and the game works fully without watching any.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). Before any ad is requested, the game shows Google's consent form where the law requires it (on first launch, after the opening story). You can change your answer at any time in the game under Settings > Ad privacy options.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the phone).
  • Removing your data. Uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored on your device. The developer keeps no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side; for data held by Google, use the controls described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
